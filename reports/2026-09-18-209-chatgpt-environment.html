007
You may have heard that using one ChatGPT prompt takes 5 litres of water. NOT TRUE.
correction
Revised down from 100 litres, and still not sourced. The highest credible published per-response figure is the Washington Post / UC Riverside estimate for a 100-word GPT-4 email: ≈519 mL on a US average and up to 1,408 mL in Washington state, counting onsite evaporation plus power-generation water. The often-quoted 500 mL comes from Li et al., who wrote 500 mL per 10–50 responses, later misquoted as per prompt. Company onsite figures are far smaller: ≈0.32 mL (OpenAI) and 0.26 mL (Google). So 5 L is roughly 3.5× above even the highest published estimate and ~15,000× the company figures. The 'NOT TRUE' verdict stands; pick a real number to knock down. Washington Post / UC Riverside · Li et al. 2023 · OpenAI figure
